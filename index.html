Our Philosophy

The Grand
Collector

Every plate tells a story. Inspired by the rigid geometry of 1920s Art Deco and the fluid, atmospheric soul of a Parisian evening, F.R.N.D.S Grand Café is not merely a restaurant — it is a curated experience.

We break the template. We embrace intentional asymmetry and editorial pacing. Our kitchen speaks two languages: the fiery embrace of the Mediterranean and the whispered precision of Japanese craftsmanship.
